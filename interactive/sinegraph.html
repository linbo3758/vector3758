From a Circle to a Sine Graph

A point moves around a unit circle. Its vertical projection becomes the y-value of the sine function.

The key idea

The point has coordinates (cos θ, sin θ) on the unit circle. Its vertical coordinate is therefore sin θ. As θ increases, recording that height produces the sine graph.

y = sin θ   →   y = sin x
