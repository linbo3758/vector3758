Constructing the SHM Displacement–Time Graph

The graph is built from the oscillator itself: measure its displacement at equal time intervals, plot each value, then reveal the continuous sinusoidal relationship.

The construction

At one time t, the mass has displacement x(t). Recording that same value on the displacement axis at the corresponding time gives one point on the graph. Repeating this at equal intervals produces the sampled waveform.
